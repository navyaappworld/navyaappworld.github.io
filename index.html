Privacy Policy for Safe PDF Viewer

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Safe PDF Viewer is developed and provided by Navya App World. This Privacy Policy explains how Safe PDF Viewer handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Safe PDF Viewer does not require you to create an account and does not operate its own backend server for storing your personal information or PDF files.

PDF files selected and opened in the application are processed locally on your device. Your PDF documents are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by Navya App World.

2. Files and Documents

The application allows you to select PDF files from your device using Android's file selection system.

PDF documents are used only to provide the application's features, including viewing, bookmarking, sharing, printing, and navigation.

Recent file information and bookmarks may be stored locally on your device so that the application can provide its history and bookmark features.

3. Advertising

Safe PDF Viewer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain information from your device for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services. This may include information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, application interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

Information collected by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by Safe PDF Viewer is used to provide and improve the application's functionality, including PDF viewing, recent files, bookmarks, sharing, printing, and related features.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob to provide, measure, and improve advertisements and related services.

5. Information Sharing

Navya App World does not sell your PDF documents or personal information.

The application uses Google AdMob, which may collect and share applicable device and usage information with Google and its partners for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

6. PDF Sharing and Printing

If you choose to share or print a PDF using features provided by the Android operating system or other applications installed on your device, the selected document may be transferred to the application or service you choose.

Such third-party applications and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

Safe PDF Viewer is designed to process PDF documents locally on your device and does not upload your PDF files to servers operated by Navya App World.

Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to the security practices of those services.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Navya App World does not maintain a server-side account or database containing your PDF documents.

Locally stored application information, such as recent file history and bookmarks, can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android device behavior.

9. Children's Privacy

Safe PDF Viewer is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Third-Party Services

Safe PDF Viewer uses third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable laws, or our practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
