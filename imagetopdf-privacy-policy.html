Privacy Policy for Image PDF Converter- JPG/PNG

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Navya App World ("we", "our", or "us") developed the Image PDF Converter- JPG/PNG application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

The Image PDF Converter- JPG/PNG app is designed to perform image and PDF conversion directly on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores the images or PDF files you select for conversion.

The app may access images and PDF files on your device only when you choose files for conversion or viewing through the app.

2. Files and Media

When you select images or PDF files, the app uses the Android file-selection system to access the files you choose. The selected files are processed locally on your device.

Converted files are saved to storage locations provided by Android, such as the Documents or Pictures folders, depending on the conversion type.

3. Conversion History

The app may store information about your recent conversion activity locally on your device so that it can display your recent activity within the app. This information is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information, including device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and advertising-related information, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable laws.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, including personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on applicable settings and consent requirements.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal files, images, or PDF documents with third parties. Your selected files are processed locally on your device.

Advertising services provided by Google AdMob may independently process information as described in Google's privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

7. Data Security

Because image and PDF conversion is performed locally on your device, your selected files are not transmitted to our servers for conversion. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and the files stored on it.

8. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Image PDF Converter- JPG/PNG, you can contact us through the developer contact information provided on Google Play.
